## Welcome to NomadBill

Create invoices and quotes, track incoming receipts, sync bank transactions, and manage bookkeeping across multiple companies from one account. NomadBill is built for freelancers, digital nomads, and global businesses that need invoicing in EUR, USD, GBP, CHF, and AED, with customer-facing documents in German, English, French, and Spanish.

NomadBill combines billing, document generation, and finance workflows in one place. You can send branded PDFs, automate parts of your bookkeeping, connect your bank with Plaid, process payments with Stripe, and organize team access as your business grows.
