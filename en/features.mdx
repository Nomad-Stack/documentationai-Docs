## What you can do with NomadBill

Create and send invoices, prepare quotes, track incoming invoices and receipts, connect bank accounts, process billing emails, design PDF templates, organize business data with custom fields, and collaborate with your team from one workspace. NomadBill focuses on the day-to-day workflows small businesses need to bill clients, capture documents, reconcile payments, and stay organized across one or more companies.

## Supported currencies and languages

Use NomadBill to issue documents in these supported currencies:

- **EUR** — Euro
- **USD** — US Dollar
- **GBP** — British Pound
- **CHF** — Swiss Franc
- **AED** — UAE Dirham

Generate invoices in these supported languages:

- **DE** — German
- **EN** — English
- **FR** — French
- **ES** — Spanish
