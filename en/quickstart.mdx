## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- A valid email address
- Your business details, including company name, address, and VAT number if applicable
- Your preferred default language and currency
- Bank account details if you plan to add payout or payment information later

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Start with a 7-day free trial. No credit card is required when you sign up.
</Callout>

## Sign up and set up your company

Create your account, then configure the company you will invoice from.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account" icon="users" title-type="p">
    Visit [app.nomadbill.co](https://app.nomadbill.co/) and sign up with your email and password, or continue with Google.

    After you confirm your account, NomadBill signs you in and takes you to company setup.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your first company" icon="plus" title-type="p">
    Enter your company name and choose a company slug. NomadBill uses the slug to identify your company workspace.

    Select a default language from `DE`, `EN`, `FR`, or `ES`, then choose your default currency: `EUR`, `USD`, `GBP`, `CHF`, or `AED`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add company details in Settings" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    Open [Settings](https://app.nomadbill.co/settings) and complete your company profile with your legal company name, address, VAT number, and bank details.

    You can also configure branding, invoice defaults, PDF templates, and custom fields from the same area.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Send your first invoice

Create your first invoice from the invoice editor, then send it as a PDF by email or download it for manual delivery.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a new invoice" icon="plus" title-type="p">
    Go to `Invoices` and click `New`, or open `/invoices/new` directly.

    NomadBill creates a draft invoice for the company you are currently working in.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the customer, currency, and language" icon="users" title-type="p">
    Choose the invoice currency and document language, then enter the customer name and address.

    Use one of the supported invoice currencies: `EUR`, `USD`, `GBP`, `CHF`, or `AED`. Use one of the supported invoice languages: `DE`, `EN`, `FR`, or `ES`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add line items and VAT" icon="edit" title-type="p">
    Add each line item with a name, quantity, and unit price. Then set the VAT percentage for the invoice or line items based on your billing needs.

    A common default is `19%` VAT. Check the totals before moving on.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview and send the PDF" icon="send" title-type="p">
    Preview the generated PDF to confirm layout, customer details, totals, and VAT.

    When everything looks correct, send the invoice by email or download the PDF and deliver it yourself. If you send it from NomadBill, the email is delivered through Resend.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  NomadBill auto-numbers invoices per company using the `R-00001` format, so your first invoice is numbered automatically.
</Callout>

## Log your first expense

Upload an incoming invoice or receipt to start tracking supplier costs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Incoming Invoices" icon="upload" title-type="p">
    Go to `Incoming Invoices` or open `/incoming`.

    This area stores uploaded files in your workspace and keeps them attached to the expense record.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload the file" icon="plus" title-type="p">
    Upload a supplier invoice or receipt as a PDF or image.

    NomadBill stores the file in the `incoming-invoices` storage bucket so you can access it again later.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete the expense details" icon="edit" title-type="p">
    Add the supplier name, amount, category, invoice date, and due date if relevant.

    After you save the record, the expense is available for review alongside the original uploaded document.
  </Step>
</Steps>
