## Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have:

- A valid email address
- Access to your business details (name, address, tax ID)
- Bank account information for payouts

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  No credit card required for the 30-day free trial. Upgrade anytime.
</Callout>

## Sign up and access your dashboard

Get your Nomadbill account set up in seconds.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create account" icon="user-plus" title-type="p">
    Visit [app.nomadbill.co](https://app.nomadbill.co/) and click Start free.

    Enter your email and create a password, or sign up with Google.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify email" icon="mail" title-type="p">
    Check your inbox for the verification link and click it to activate your account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete profile" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    Add your business name, default currency (e.g., EUR), and address.

    Select your home country for tax compliance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Send your first invoice

Create and send professional invoices instantly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start new invoice" icon="plus" title-type="p">
    Click New Invoice on the dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add client and details" icon="user" title-type="p">
    Enter client name, email, and items:

    ```markdown
    Brand identity retainer | €3,200.00
    Web design landing     | €1,450.00
    Total                  | €4,962.00
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set currency and send" icon="send" title-type="p">
    Choose client currency (auto-converts at ECB rates). Preview and click Send.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Enable recurring billing for retainers—Nomadbill handles reminders and retries.
</Callout>

## Log your first expense

Track expenses across borders effortlessly.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Quick Log" icon="plus-circle">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select category" title-type="p">
        From dashboard, click New Expense → Coworking (CDMX, $18).
      </Step>

      <Step title="Add receipt" title-type="p">
        Upload photo or enter amount manually. Auto-categorizes and reconciles.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Bank Import" icon="arrow-down-circle">
    Connect your bank account via Plaid integration.

    Nomadbill pulls transactions and matches to invoices automatically.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
