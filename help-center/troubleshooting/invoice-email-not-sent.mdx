---
title: Invoice Email Not Sent
description: Troubleshoot invoice delivery issues in Nomadbill.
---

## Symptoms
- Client didn't receive invoice email
- No delivery confirmation in history

## Quick Fix
<Steps>
  <Step title="Check client email address">
    Ensure the email on the invoice matches your client's email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resend from dashboard">
    Open the invoice and click "Resend Email".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check spam/junk folders">
    Ask your client to look for emails from `no-reply@nomadbill.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact support">
    If still not delivered, email support@nomadbill.com with invoice ID.
  </Step>
</Steps>
