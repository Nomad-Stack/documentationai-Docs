## Add Your First Client

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Clients tab">
    From your dashboard, click "Clients" in the main menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Client">
    Select the "Add Client" button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter details">
    Fill in the client name, email, company, and country.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click "Save" to add them. Your new client is ready for invoicing!
  </Step>
</Steps>
