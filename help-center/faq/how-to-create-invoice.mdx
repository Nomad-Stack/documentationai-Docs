---
title: How do I create an invoice?
description: Easy steps to create and send invoices in Nomadbill.
---

## Steps

1. Log in to your Nomadbill dashboard.
2. Click "Create Invoice" at the top right.
3. Add client details, invoice items, and due date.
4. Review totals and taxes (Nomadbill auto-calculates for you).
5. Click "Preview" to check your invoice.
6. Hit "Send"—your client will receive it by email.

**Tip:**
Invoices save automatically as drafts until sent. You can edit drafts at any time before sending.
